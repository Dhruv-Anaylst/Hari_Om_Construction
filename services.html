What we do

Four service lines, one point of contact

From laying pipe to staffing the crew that maintains it — scoped, resourced and supervised by one company.

Infrastructure

Pipeline & CGD Infrastructure

End-to-end erection, testing and commissioning of steel and MDPE pipeline networks — from cross-country transmission sections to City Gas Distribution networks reaching domestic, commercial and industrial consumers.

  • Right-of-way clearance & trenching
  • Steel & MDPE pipe laying and welding
  • Tie-ins, hydro-testing & commissioning
  • Associated civil, mechanical & telecom works
People

Manpower Supply

A deployable bench of trained site personnel — mobilised on short notice for new construction, shutdowns, or standing O&M contracts, and managed under our own site supervision.

  • Welders, fitters & pipeline riggers
  • Site supervisors & safety officers
  • Shutdown & turnaround crews
  • Long-term staffing for CGD operators
Maintenance

Operations & Maintenance

Ongoing O&M support for CGD networks and pipeline assets already in service — routine inspection, emergency repair response, and scheduled shutdown coverage.

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance
  • Emergency repair response
  • Shutdown & overhaul support
  • Network modernisation works
Oversight

Project Management

Site supervision and HSE compliance that runs alongside the client's own project team — one accountable point of contact for progress, safety and schedule.

  • Site supervision & daily progress reporting
  • HSE compliance & documentation
  • Scheduling & resource planning
  • Client liaison through to handover

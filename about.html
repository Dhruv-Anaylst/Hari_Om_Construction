Who we are

Built on fieldwork, not just paperwork

Hariom Construction. is a gas pipeline infrastructure and manpower services company working with clients across India's oil & gas sector.

Our profile

Hariom Construction was set up to close the gap between pipeline construction and the manpower it depends on — one contractor for the trench work and the crews that staff it. We work on cross-country pipeline sections and City Gas Distribution networks, and support operators with the O&M staffing needed to keep those networks running day to day. What guides us

Three things every site runs on

Safety first

Site work follows documented HSE procedures, with PPE, permits and toolbox talks treated as non-negotiable, not paperwork.

On-time execution

Pipeline work runs on tight regulatory and shutdown windows — our crews plan to those windows, not around them.

Skilled workforce

Welders, fitters and riggers who already know pipeline work — deployed with supervision, not left to figure it out on site.

OUR COMPANY

A responsive construction partner for critical pipeline networks.

Based in Rohini, Delhi, Hari Om Construction supports work across pipeline and City Gas Distribution infrastructure, skilled manpower deployment, operations and maintenance, and project coordination.

We understand that dependable execution is built through preparation, clear responsibilities, site awareness and steady communication. Our aim is to work alongside clients and project teams with a practical, accountable approach from mobilisation through handover.
